Daily Companion — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Daily Companion mobile app ("the App"), published by Kontio Labs, an app studio of Kontio Power, LLC ("we," "us," "our"), handles your information. The short version: your personal data stays on your device.

No accounts, no tracking, no ads

The App does not require an account or sign-in. It contains no third-party advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. We cannot see your journals, habits, moods, or anything else you enter.

Data stored on your device

Everything you create in the App — gratitude and reflection entries, habits and completions, mood check-ins, saved favorites, priorities, preferences, and reminder settings — is stored locally on your device only. If you delete the App, that data is deleted with it. You can also erase everything at any time in Settings → Delete all my data.

Backups are yours

The App can export your data as a single backup file. That file is created on your device and shared only where you choose to send it (your email, cloud drive, etc.). We never receive a copy.

Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device using the operating system's notification service. No push-notification servers are involved, and notification content never leaves your phone.

AI personalization (optional, subscribers only)

If you use the optional AI personalization feature, the App sends a short activity summary to our secure service to generate your personalized content: your first name (if you chose to provide one), your best habit streak (name and count), your recent average mood score, your count of gratitude entries, and the day's verse or affirmation being personalized. These summaries are used only to generate your content, are not used to train AI models, and are not sold or shared.

AI Coach chat (optional, opt-in)

The Coach is an optional chat assistant. Before your first conversation the App asks for explicit consent, because each coach message includes a snapshot of your recent activity so the coach can give personal guidance: habit progress and streaks, mood check-ins, today's priorities, your last few gratitude entries, and the day's verse and affirmation — plus the messages you type.

That data goes to whichever AI service you chose:

  • Your linked Claude account — requests go directly from your device to Anthropic under your own account and credentials, governed by Anthropic's privacy policy. Your credential is stored only on your device, is excluded from backups, and can be unlinked anytime in Settings.
  • Our secure service — used only to generate the reply; not used to train models, not sold, not shared.

Conversations are stored on your device only, and you can clear them or delete everything at any time. If you never open the Coach or never consent, none of this data leaves your phone.

Purchases & subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) — we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat, a subscription-management service, to validate purchases; it processes anonymized purchase tokens and device identifiers as described in its privacy policy.

Children's privacy

The App is rated 4+/Everyone and suitable for a general audience. Because the App collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Because your data never reaches us, most data-rights requests can be satisfied directly on your device: view your data in the App, export it via backup, and erase it via Settings → Delete all my data. We do not sell or share personal information. For anything else, contact us below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted, and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Use our contact form. Publisher: Kontio Power, LLC.
