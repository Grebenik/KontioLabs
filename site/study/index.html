Study

Spaced repetition flashcards, right in your browser. Pick a topic to review the cards due today — the SM-2 algorithm schedules each card's next review, and your progress is saved privately on this device.

New here? Here's how it works

1
Pick a topic

Choose a deck below and click it to start today's session.

2
Flip the card

Read the question, then tap the card (or press Space) to reveal the answer.

3
Rate yourself honestly

Again / Hard / Good / Easy — your answer decides when you'll see that card again.

4
Come back daily

New cards are capped per day (set the limit below); due reviews queue up on their own.

Want to review the whole deck instead of just what's due? Switch to All cards inside any session. Progress is saved only on this device/browser — nothing is uploaded.

Heads up: your browser is blocking local storage (private browsing?). You can still study, but progress won't be saved between visits.
Large decks introduce this many unseen cards each day, on top of scheduled reviews.
