About Kontio Labs

Kontio Labs is a small, independent app studio. We build focused mobile apps that do one job well — and get out of your way. No bloat, no dark patterns, no harvesting your data to sell it on.

What we make

We design and ship native-quality apps for iOS and Android. Our first app, Financial Freedom, is a private personal-finance tracker that helps you map a path out of debt and into savings — with everything stored on your own device. More apps are in development.

How we build

  • Privacy by default — where an app doesn't need an account or the cloud, it won't ask for one. Your data stays with you.
  • Small and focused — each app solves a clear problem instead of trying to be everything.
  • Honest pricing — free to start, with no manipulative upsells.
  • Actively maintained — we ship real updates and listen to the people who use our apps.

The company

Kontio Labs is the app studio of Kontio Power, LLC. Everything you see here is built and operated by the same small team, so feedback reaches the people who can actually act on it.
