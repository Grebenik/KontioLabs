Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kontio Labs ("we," "us," "our"), an app studio of Kontio Power, LLC, collects, uses and protects information when you visit kontiolabs.com (the "Site") or use our apps. By using the Site or our apps, you agree to this policy.

Our apps store data on your device

Our apps are designed to be privacy-first. Where an app does not need an account or the cloud to work, your data (such as the financial figures you enter in Financial Freedom) is stored locally on your own device and is not transmitted to us. Backups you create are yours to keep and control. Any exception for a specific app will be disclosed in that app's store listing.

Information we collect on this website

  • Information you provide — via our contact form (name, email, message). Used only to respond to your enquiry.
  • Automatically collected data — standard technical data (IP address, browser, device type, pages visited) via our analytics provider, in aggregate and anonymised form.

Cookies & analytics

The Site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate, anonymised traffic patterns so we can improve the Site. It may set cookies and process data as described in Google's privacy documentation. We do not use it to identify individual visitors, and we do not display third-party advertising on this Site. You can control or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Your rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to or restrict processing, and opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information under laws such as the EU/UK GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). We do not sell personal information. To exercise your rights, contact us below.

Children's privacy

The Site and our apps are intended for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Data retention & security

We keep contact messages only as long as needed to respond and maintain a reasonable record. We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Third-party links & app stores

The Site links to third-party services, including the Apple App Store and Google Play. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices — review their policies separately.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted, and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Use our contact form. Publisher: Kontio Power, LLC.
